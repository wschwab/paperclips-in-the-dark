## 1. Approved behavior

**Game mode belongs to one scoundrel, never the campaign or crew.** Two
scoundrels in the same crew can be in different modes. Choosing a mode changes
only the current scoundrel’s sheet assistance, not anybody else’s state.

The three equal choices are **Free Play**, **Score**, and **Downtime**. They
are non-linear: choose any one at any time, including leaving a popup or
Resolution halfway through. There is no required cycle, prerequisite mode,
or table-wide phase advancement.

The mode switcher is always visible: a sticky left-gutter panel on wide screens
and a footer on narrow screens. It suggests relevant work; it never determines
whether an operation is legal. **No operation is rejected because of mode.**
This applies to character, crew, clock, and batch operations alike.

The real sheet keeps **every section in every mode**, including **Personal**.
The mockups simplify the background and show only what changes. Score makes
Loadout prominent and collapses Projects and Contacts into expandable
sections; neither their contents nor their operations disappear.

### Start a Score: Planning → Loadout → Engagement

These are three assistance popups, not three additional modes or server gates.
The switcher remains available throughout.

1. **Planning.** Choose Assault, Deception, Stealth, Occult, Social, or Transport.
   Keep the SRD descriptions. **No detail-prompt field**: no point of
   infiltration, point of attack, or equivalent input in this popup.
2. **Loadout.** Choose Light, Normal, or Heavy and select items. Limits come
   from `data/games/blades-in-the-dark.json`,
   `LoadMaxima.CommitmentMaxBulk`: Light **3**, Normal **5**, Heavy **6**.
   They are settings-derived, not new literals in application code. Items and
   bulk come from the game/playbook data; the Lurk’s Fine lockpicks have 0 bulk.
   Choosing items here is assistance: more items can be declared during play.
3. **Engagement.** **The crew makes the engagement roll**, not the GM on its
   behalf. Remind the table to start with 1d for sheer luck, add 1d for each
   major advantage, and subtract 1d for each major disadvantage. Cover the
   operation, the plan’s detail and target defenses/vulnerabilities, helpful
   friends/contacts, interfering enemies/rivals, Tier, and other circumstances.
   The GM frames the resulting first obstacle. The sheet neither rolls dice
   nor requires a result to continue.

The Score sheet then shows the **committed loadout**. A small **Change loadout**
control opens a confirmation step before replacing it. Back/Close cancels the
proposed replacement; confirmation commits it. This is not a mode restriction.

### Score: desperate-action XP and End Score

The panel contains **+ Desperate action XP** and **End Score**.
For a desperate action, the player chooses the attribute used and marks 1 XP
in that attribute immediately, including a desperate group action. This uses
ordinary attribute-XP operations; it is not deferred to Resolution and is not
awarded automatically again there.

### End Score → Resolution

End Score opens **Resolution**, with a six-step stepper visible at every step:

**Scoundrel XP → Payoff → Heat → Entanglements → Crew XP → Crew Rep**.

Every step has **Back** and **Next**, with Next visually primary, and a
**confirm with your GM** note. Back/Next changes the displayed step, not game
rewards. Selecting a step, leaving the flow, or returning to a step never
replays a mutation. Resolution is a workflow within Score, **not a fourth
mode**. Its final Next offers Downtime for this scoundrel only.

| Step | Approved content and controls |
|---|---|
| **Scoundrel XP** | The three expression questions appear **only here**: playbook-specific challenge (Lurk: stealth or evasion), expressed beliefs/drives/heritage/background, and struggled with vice/traumas. For each trigger that occurred, mark at least 1 XP; 2 if it occurred a lot. No trigger means no automatic minimum award. Per-track mark buttons for Playbook, Insight, Prowess, and Resolve. This is an end-of-session review offered at score resolution, not a new mandatory per-score award. |
| **Payoff** | Crew receives 2 rep ± target/crew Tier difference, minimum zero; a completely quiet score earns zero rep. Coin guide: 2 minor, 4 small, 6 standard, 8 big, 10+ major. If owed, tithe is crew Tier + 1 coin. Show crew receipts, **direct crew rep and crew coin editors**, and the separate editable **my share · coin** field for this scoundrel. Never automatically award the full crew payout to every scoundrel. |
| **Heat** | Base heat is **0 smooth/quiet, 2 contained, 4 loud/chaotic, 6 wild**. Add +1 for a high-profile/well-connected target, +1 hostile turf, +1 at war, +2 if killing was involved, whoever did it. Include the **direct crew heat editor**. The agreed heat belongs to the crew, not this scoundrel. |
| **Entanglements** | Show current crew heat; the illustration is **4**. Initially show **only Heat 4–5**: 1–3 Gang Trouble or Questioning; 4–5 Reprisals or Unquiet Dead; 6 Show of Force. A small **Show all** disclosure reveals the full SRD table. Roll dice equal to wanted level and use the highest die; at wanted level zero, roll two dice and keep the lowest. Outcomes are handled at the table, not generated or enforced by the server. |
| **Crew XP — OPTIONAL** | A **direct crew XP editor** on the step, with Update crew XP and **Skip as equal choices**. No required trip to the crew sheet. |
| **Crew Rep — OPTIONAL** | A **direct crew rep editor** with an equal Skip choice. Remind the player to skip if rep was already recorded during Payoff: this is not a second award. |

Every direct crew editor, including Payoff, Heat, Crew XP, and Crew Rep, shows
this exact warning:

> **Only one player should update the crew — check nobody else already has.**

Crew updates are explicit, separately targeted crew mutations. Advancing the
scoundrel’s stepper never applies them automatically. An unlinked scoundrel
can still use every mode and pass through Resolution; offer crew linking or
skip the crew edit rather than blocking the flow.

### Downtime: a soft activity budget

The panel shows **●●○**, **2 of 2 left** in the illustration. The free-activity
allowance comes from settings; two is the displayed example, **not a hard cap**.
Provide **+1 (coin)**, **+1 (gift)**, and **−1** adjustments.

Activity buttons are **Acquire Asset, Long-Term Project, Recover, Reduce Heat,
Train, Indulge Vice**, plus **Other**. Each explicit activity selection spends
one budget unit. Helping someone does not spend an activity; in particular,
healing someone else is their Recover activity, not the healer’s.

The counter may reach zero or go negative. Explain the over-budget state and
suggest an adjustment; **do not reject spending, disable activity buttons, or
block the underlying sheet operation**. Switching modes must not reset the
budget. Buying or gifting an activity and recording an activity are explicit
actions, not side effects of every project, healing, training, or vice mutation.

On narrow screens the activity menu is in the sheet above the fixed mode
footer, as shown in the approved narrow mockup.

Gift adjustments and Other are proposed tracker controls, not additional SRD

approved surface shows coin/gift controls. It does not compute fictional
eligibility, dice results, treatment, entanglement consequences, or incarceration.

## 2. Existing contract seams

The following are current contract facts, not implemented game-mode behavior:

- The character DTO retains identity, revision, dossier, monitor, talent,
  playbook, gear, session and lifecycle state. GAME-MODE-01 adds stored mode,
  workflow and budget directly to `contract/schemas/character.json`.
- End Score retains out-of-action cleanup when Resolution begins. **Stress
  clears only when completely full; otherwise it persists. Committed loadout
  is retained by default.** The existing optional `resetLoadoutCommitment`
  flag remains supported and defaults to false; explicit true opts into the
  existing cleanup and locked-commitment error. Only the original partial-stress
  assertion in `LIFECYCLE-ENDSCORE-002` requires amendment.
- Existing `end-downtime` clears session expressions and accepts caller-supplied
  vice relief; it is not a mode switch:
  `endDowntime` in `contract/openapi.yaml`.
- Existing mutation routes include attribute/playbook XP, personal fund gain/
  spend, and crew heat, rep, XP, and coin operations:
  `contract/openapi.yaml`.
- `OperationResult` retains `ok`, `applied`, `sideEffects`, `error`, returned
  entities, and derived undo metadata. GAME-MODE-01 adds conditional `mode` and
  advisory `suggestedOps` in `contract/schemas/operation-result.json`, not a
  parallel result envelope.
- The error envelope and HTTP mapping in `contract/openapi.yaml` and
  `contract/schemas/operation-result.json` are unchanged.

The new design is a sheet aid layered onto those operations. It does not need
a campaign-state resource, campaign-mode storage, a roster-wide transition,
or a campaign-level score-ending composite.

## 3. Frozen per-scoundrel API contract

GAME-MODE-01 declares these fields/routes in the contract. This is **not a claim
that the current server implements them**. API/domain naming remains
`character`; human-facing copy remains **scoundrel**.

### Character state and operation-result assistance

Add `mode` to the character, with `free-play`, `score`, or `downtime`. Default
new and migrated scoundrels to `free-play`. Persist this state in the existing
character document using its revision, atomic write, snapshots, and undo;
**no new campaign resource or revision**.

Stored workflow/budget fields (illustrative state):

```json
{
  "mode": "score",
  "modeFlow": {
    "kind": "resolution",
    "step": "payoff"
  },
  "downtime": {
    "free": 2,
    "paid": 0,
    "spent": 0,
    "activities": []
  }
}
```

`modeFlow.kind` is `none`, `score-start`, or `resolution`. `none` has an empty
step string; score-start steps are `planning`, `loadout`, `engagement`;
resolution steps are `scoundrel-xp`, `payoff`, `heat`, `entanglements`,
`crew-xp`, `crew-rep`. These cursors describe a displayed workflow, **not an
operation-eligibility phase**. Back, Next, or direct step selection can move
freely without requiring earlier work to be completed.

Store mode, workflow, and budget state **directly in `character.json`**. The
budget counters are stored state: `free` snapshots
`DowntimeSettings.FreeActivities` at initialization/completed-score reset;
`paid` records explicit extras (including gifts) and signed corrections;
`spent` records completed activity spends. The available budget is
**`free + paid - spent`**, which may be negative. No maximum is declared.
Activity records contain `{activity, description}`. Mode/flow changes leave
these counters untouched. A changed settings allowance takes effect on the
next explicit budget reset, not by rewriting an existing budget during a read.

`GET /api/characters/{id}` retains its existing **Character** schema. There is
**no separate `characterRead` projection** or transient budget DTO.
Add advisory **`suggestedOps` only to `operation-result.json`**, beside the
existing result fields and current character `mode`. Suggestions are not
persisted and **not an allowlist**. Score can suggest attribute XP, gear, and
End Score; Downtime can suggest activity tracking and existing sheet mutations.
Resolution suggestions can follow the displayed step without excluding any op.

Crew responses remain crew responses: they do not acquire a shared crew/campaign
mode. A scoundrel flow reads its own mode separately when editing the crew.

### Operation surface

All new character routes use the existing op-style POST pattern under
`/api/characters/{id}` and the existing mutation header conventions.

| Operation / proposed route | Request and effect |
|---|---|
| `mode.set` — `POST /api/characters/{id}/ops/mode.set` | `{mode}`. Any of the three choices from any current mode. Entering Score offers Planning; leaving a workflow clears its active cursor but does not apply cleanup, XP, crew changes, or a budget reset. Selecting an already-current mode does not restart bookkeeping. |
| `mode.flow.set` — `POST /api/characters/{id}/ops/mode.flow.set` | `{kind, step}` from the declared workflow enums. Saves the assistance cursor for Back/Next/direct step selection. Selecting a Score workflow establishes Score for this scoundrel rather than failing because its previous mode differed. No completion prerequisites. |
| **`end-score` — existing `POST /api/characters/{id}/end-score`** | Atomically enters **Resolution / Scoundrel XP**, clears out-of-action flags, and clears stress **only if completely full**. Partial stress persists. **Committed loadout is retained by default.** Preserve optional `resetLoadoutCommitment` (default false); explicit true retains existing opt-in cleanup/lock checks. The approved UI omits it. Reset this scoundrel’s budget after the completed score. No automatic XP/crew award or other-scoundrel change; no mode error outside Score. |
| `downtime.spend` — `POST /api/characters/{id}/ops/downtime.spend` | `{activity, description}`; activity is one of the six menu names or Other. Append the explicit activity record and increment stored `spent` by one. Available in every mode and even when `free + paid - spent` is zero/negative; no automatic mode change or activity outcome. |
| `downtime.adjust` — `POST /api/characters/{id}/ops/downtime.adjust` | `{delta, reason}` with reasons coin, gift, correction. +1 (coin) records an **already-paid** extra activity in stored `paid`; +1 (gift) likewise adds an available activity. Neither charges coin or rep. −1 is an explicit correction. Available in every mode. |
| `downtime.reset` — `POST /api/characters/{id}/ops/downtime.reset` | Snapshots free activities from settings and clears stored paid/spent/activity records. Successful End Score resets this budget after the completed score in the same character mutation. Mode changes, popup resume, and completing Resolution never reset it again. |

Completing Resolution selects Downtime for this scoundrel using `mode.set`;
leaving early can do the same without any forced awards. Step navigation and
mode selection are not aliases for `end-downtime` or other lifecycle cleanup.
Do not add a new mandatory End Downtime wizard to the approved design.

Planning choice is UI assistance, committed gear uses the existing gear model,
and Engagement has no roll/result requirement. Use existing gear mutations
and their revision/confirmation semantics for the loadout popup and confirmed
replacement; do not create another inventory or dice service.

### Crew and personal edits

The crew editors target the linked crew’s ID and revision, using existing crew
operations for rep, coin, heat, and XP. The UI must explicitly distinguish an
amount added from a displayed total, translate edits according to each
existing operation’s semantics, and refresh after each successful mutation.
The editable **my share** targets this scoundrel’s personal fund, not the crew.
No hidden fan-out awards or shared phase transitions occur.

Every crew write sends the observed crew revision with `If-Match`; use the
existing `Idempotency-Key` support for a retry of the same intended mutation.
On `STALE_REVISION`, refresh and ask the player to reconcile the crew value;
do not blindly replay a delta. The one-player warning is important because
revision checks cannot detect two different players intentionally recording
the same payoff sequentially. Returning to Crew Rep after Payoff must not
silently award it twice.

Each entity edit retains its own operation/snapshot/undo semantics. Back/Next
is navigation, not a campaign transaction or a rollback of already-confirmed
crew edits. Activity tracking is explicit: the client must not silently call
`downtime.spend` after every otherwise-available sheet mutation.

### Errors and responses: existing envelope only

Use the existing **`OperationResult` / `OperationError` envelope only**.
No new HTTP statuses, no new mode/budget error codes, and no parallel error
shape. Unknown enums or malformed request bodies use existing `VALIDATION`;
missing entities, stale revisions, and other existing failures retain their
current codes, typed details, recovery instructions, and statuses.

**Delete the old mode-gating table from the design.** There is no `WRONG_MODE`,
no exhausted-budget error, and no phase-based `allowedOps` enforcement.
A valid operation does not fail because its mode is unsuggested, its workflow
step differs, or its soft activity allowance is spent. The same rule applies
inside `campaign.batch`.

This does not abolish unrelated existing structural, lifecycle, inventory,
fund, or confirmation rules. Those must keep their existing error contracts;
a mode suggestion is never another reason to reject or disable an operation.
Pure `mode.set` changes assistance, not gameplay rewards or lifecycle state.

Response assistance must reflect the latest returned character. On failures,
include it only where the existing envelope can supply current character
state; do not invent a mode on a missing/unreadable entity. The contract card
must specify conditional presence for operation results and existing failure
families; plain character reads retain the canonical Character shape. Generate
agent references from the resulting contract rather than hand-authoring a second eligibility policy.

## 4. Frozen human decisions — GAME-MODE-01

All three previously open decisions are resolved:

1. **End Score cleanup occurs when Resolution begins.** Clear out-of-action
   state then. Clear stress only if the meter was completely full; retain
   partial stress. **The approved UI does not clear the committed loadout.**
   Preserve existing optional `resetLoadoutCommitment`, default false, for
   callers explicitly requesting that cleanup. The character mutation,
   Resolution entry, and completed-score budget reset remain atomic and undoable.
2. **The activity budget is uncapped.** `DowntimeSettings.FreeActivities` comes
   from game settings: the human authorized **2** per the SRD in every game
   file sharing the settings schema. Signed remaining may be negative; spending
   never fails because the soft allowance is exhausted. Reset the budget
   **after a completed score**, never on a mode switch or on navigating
   Resolution. The schema declares **no maximum**. Only game-settings JSON and
   its validating definitions are in scope; `campaign-data/` is off-limits.
3. **+1 (coin) records an already-paid extra activity.** It adds one to this
   scoundrel’s available activities and **never automatically charges coin**.
   Gifts behave the same way without implying a payment. Fund/crew balance
   mutations remain separate explicit actions; the tracker does not require
   funds or introduce an insufficient-funds check.

There are **no remaining product questions**. Workflow cursors are persisted
per-character assistance for resume/agent visibility, not eligibility phases.
Incarceration/cohort automation and campaign resources remain out of scope.


## 5. Updated card breakdown

GAME-MODE-01 freezes the decisions above into the contract and adds new red
oracles. Backend, frontend, and conformance then implement/test the same approved
behavior; no worker substitutes the former campaign-wide design.

### Card 1 — Contract change: per-scoundrel state and advisory API

- Add stored character mode, workflow cursor, activity records and free/paid/spent
  budget counters, canonical defaults, and import/repair migration.
- Add `mode.set`, cursor navigation, `downtime.spend`, adjustment/reset contracts;

- Update generated API/agent references and this proposal’s frozen decisions.

### Card 2 — Backend: character state, budget bookkeeping, and suggestions

- Store assistance on the existing character; normalize/migrate legacy documents
  through the normal contract-defined path. Reuse atomic writes and revisions.

  project current mode/suggestions without persisting derived suggestions.

### Card 3 — Frontend: approved sheet assistance and direct editors

- Build the sticky gutter/narrow footer with three equal non-linear choices;

- Implement Planning → Loadout → Engagement, committed loadout display and

- Build the six-step Resolution stepper with Back/primary Next and GM notes;
  questions only on Scoundrel XP, direct crew editors with the exact warning,
  separate my-share coin, heat-filtered entanglements with Show all, and optional
  equally skippable Crew XP/Rep. Navigation must never replay rewards.
- Build the settings-driven soft activity counter, adjustments, seven activity
  choices, and helping note. No zero-budget or mode-based disabled controls.
- Handle independent character/crew revisions and stale edits; keep ordinary
  sheet operations available. Include inconspicuous **SRD ↗** links.
- Use the approved captures as the visual reference. Exercise real Chromium at
  wide/narrow viewports, accessibility/themes, and the repository’s independent
  visual review gate before calling implementation complete.

### Card 4 — Conformance: advisory modes, not gating

- Character isolation: changing one scoundrel never changes another or the crew.
  Cover defaults/migration, every mode-to-mode pair, and same-mode no reset.
- Cover end-score opening Resolution, agreed cleanup atomicity, free cursor
  navigation, resume, and no XP/crew mutation from navigation or mode changes.
- Run existing operation examples across all three modes and workflow cursors,
  including batch members: no mode-based errors; unrelated existing errors keep
  their exact envelope/status/recovery semantics.
- Exercise free-activity settings variations, signed remaining, spending at
  zero/below zero, adjustments, completed-score reset, no automatic payment, undo, and retry.
- Verify stored mode/budget state on reads and advisory `mode`/`suggestedOps`
  on operation results; suggestions never become a hidden allowlist.
- Cover character/crew revision separation and exactly-once retry of explicit
  writes. UI/browser acceptance additionally verifies no duplicate crew award,
  cancel/confirm loadout behavior, Personal visibility, and soft budget controls.
