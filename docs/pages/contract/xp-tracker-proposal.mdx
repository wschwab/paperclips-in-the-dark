## Approved Score layout

The separate **XP Tracker** appears **only in Score**, with **Desperate action
XP**, **Playbook expressions**, **Scoundrel expressions**, and **Struggle
expressions**. It is not a new scoundrel-sheet section or a fourth game mode.
At widths **1100px and above**, use a distinct panel under/next to Game mode.
Below **1100px**, the fixed footer has a small **XP Tracker** button opening a
bottom sheet. Reuse the existing game-mode `mockup.css` imports, fonts, theme
controls and 06d Resolution styling, with Nyelle Vale / Wren, the Lurk.

The sheet still contains **Personal**, the **playbook XP track**, all
**attribute XP tracks**, and every other existing section. Moving session
controls into the tracker does not remove advancement controls or create a
mode gate. This is per-scoundrel assistance, never campaign-wide state.

Expression controls must faithfully match the incumbent scoundrel sheet, not
merely suggest boxes: `.stress` contains a `.stress-track` group of
`.stress-box` buttons using `data-stress` filled/empty states, followed by
`.stress-label.lbl` **below the boxes**. Preserve the current/max number and
steppers alongside the track. Use `frontend/src/components/stress-track.ts`,
`frontend/src/pages/character-sections/talents.ts:136–197` and the `.stress*`
rules in `frontend/styles/components.css` as visual truth; do not replace them
with a custom box row and an `h3` label. **All three expression rows, including
Struggle, must be fully visible in the initial 1440×1000 wide capture**,
without clipping them inside a scrolling rail.

### Compact interaction and accessibility

- Use a real button with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, not a draggable
  label masquerading as a control. Tap/click and Enter/Space toggle the sheet.
- Provide a named sheet, visible focus, an explicit Close button and Escape
  dismissal. Return focus to the opener. An expanded modal sheet contains
  focus and makes the background inert.
- Dragging the handle adds pointer/touch convenience; every open/close action
  remains possible without dragging. Content scrolling must not inadvertently
  become a dismiss gesture.
- Reserve space for the fixed footer and safe-area inset; keep controls
  reachable at 390px. Respect reduced motion without disabling interaction.

## Resolution: explicit conversion, then clear

Keep the existing six steps:

**Scoundrel XP → Payoff → Heat → Entanglements → Crew XP → Crew Rep**.

Retain Back/Next, direct step selection, confirm-with-GM guidance, separate
crew-targeted editors and optional Crew XP/Crew Rep skips. Resolution remains
within Score; choosing another mode, closing/resuming the flow or revisiting
a step must not replay an award.

**Desperate action XP is granted during play.** Mark 1 in the rolled action's
attribute immediately, including desperate group actions. That XP is already
on the permanent attribute track and is not included again in conversion.

**Expression counters are session reminders, not XP grants.** For each
trigger record 1 if it happened, 2 if it happened a lot, zero if it did not.
Scoundrel XP reviews these reminders and offers **four separate allocation
steppers: Playbook, Trait Insight, Trait Prowess and Trait Resolve**. Splitting
between destinations is explicitly supported. The illustrative expression
total **2 + 1 + 1 = 4** is not restricted to one selected track. This is the
SRD's end-of-session review offered at score resolution, not an automatic
award for each score or a mandatory reset whenever a score ends.

### Available XP, Adjust and destination room

- Show **live XP available** and **unallocated XP**: the GM-confirmed total
  minus the sum of all four allocations. Update both as the total or steppers
  change.
- The GM-confirmed total is **locked by default**. The explicit **Adjust**
  toggle unlocks editing, then locks it again when switched off. Accept a
  **nonnegative integer**, including zero; do not invent a hard maximum or
  cap the GM total at the session-expression maximum.
- Each destination shows **actual filled and empty boxes**, visually distinct
  **pending allocation preview boxes**, and numbers for **current/max,
  allocated XP and room**. Use settings-derived maxima from
  `xp-tracker-data.json`; room is `max − current`, not an illustrative fixed
  cap. A full track has zero room.
- **Block completion until every available point is placed and every
  allocation fits its destination's room.** Also block allocations whose sum
  exceeds the available total. Explain unplaced XP or insufficient room;
  never discard XP, silently clamp an award or clear counters to conceal
  overflow. Preserve pending choices and counters while blocked. Any
  advancement needed to make room belongs to later implementation.

**Only successful explicit Scoundrel XP completion converts the four
allocations and then clears the three session expression counters.** Show a
clear notice explaining this timing. Permanent playbook and attribute XP,
including already-granted desperate XP, are retained; their clears are
advancement operations, not session cleanup. Opening, closing, revisiting
the step or navigating Back/Next alone never awards or clears. Remove the old
sheet **End downtime clears expressions** control and responsibility in the
later implementation. Nothing in this mockup changes today's End Downtime API.

### Does clearing at Resolution need a contract change?

**The explicit clearing primitive already exists:** XP-add operations followed
by `session.set` with all three zeros can represent conversion/clear without
a new endpoint. But those separate operations do not define an atomic,
replay-safe completion or a persisted allocation record. **Do not add hidden
awards or clearing to `mode.flow.set`**: its contract explicitly forbids them.

**The approved full clean cutover requires later contract work.** Removing
End Downtime's public `clearSessionExpressions` responsibility changes an
existing declared behavior and frozen composite oracle. The implementation
card must update contract, backend, all callers and affected tests together,
and define Scoundrel XP completion semantics. This proposal does not
implement that change or claim it already exists.

## Recommended completion and remaining implementation questions

**Recommend one atomic server completion operation** that validates the
GM-confirmed total and all four allocations against current destination room,
credits every allocated point and only then clears the three session
expression counters. This is a **proposal only**, not an endpoint implemented
by these mockups. `mode.flow.set` must remain a cursor with no award or clear.

The earlier capacity question is **resolved by the approved human decision**:
all XP must be placed within available room; block completion instead of
discarding or silently clamping uncredited XP. Separate existing mutations
do not establish atomic completion merely because the primitives exist.

Remaining implementation questions are the exact completion wire schema and
replay/concurrency/snapshot semantics, and how later advancement operations
make room while preserving unconverted session XP. Placement, separate
destination allocation, live totals, Adjust locking, capacity blocking,
retained sheet sections, six steps and successful-completion clear timing
are decided and are not reopened here.

## Decided: atomic Scoundrel XP completion (`xp.resolve`)

Decided under XP-TRACKER-02 (authorized contract change). The op is
`POST /characters/{id}/ops/xp.resolve`, operationId `characterXpResolve`,
disposition `human` (sheet-management), `x-snapshot: true`.

- **Body:** `total` (integer ≥ 0) and `allocations` with all four keys required:
  `Playbook`, `Insight`, `Prowess`, `Resolve`, each an integer ≥ 0. The attribute
  names are the Blades settings names. Maxima come from the character's
  settings-derived tracks, never literals.
- **Effect, one revisioned write and one snapshot:** add each allocation to its
  track, then set `playbookExpressions`, `characterExpressions` and
  `struggleExpressions` to 0. Nothing else changes: not mode, not the flow cursor,
  not desperate XP.
- **Sum rule (decision):** the allocations must sum to `total`. The card left this
  open. Without it the server could not enforce "no discard, no invented XP". The
  sum is checked against the GM total, not against the session counters, so the
  total stays free.
- **Zero total (decision):** allowed. It awards nothing. On success it still clears
  the three counters, because the GM explicitly chose the total. The Resolution UI
  must say that the counters are cleared without a matching award.
- **Overflow and invalid values (decision):** an allocation beyond a track's room, a
  negative or non-integer value, or a sum that differs from `total` returns
  **HTTP 400 `VALIDATION`** with issue pointers, and changes nothing, including the
  revision. The existing codes do not fit a room overflow; no new code is added.
- **Revision and idempotency:** `If-Match` is required in the same way as the other
  character ops, and a stale revision is `STALE_REVISION` 409. `Idempotency-Key`
  follows the existing operation pattern, so a replay does not award XP twice.
- **Unchanged:** `end-downtime` keeps `clearSessionExpressions` in the API. Only its
  button leaves the sheet, in XP-TRACKER-04.

The conformance tests are `conformance/suites/semantics/xp-resolve.test.ts`
(XP-RESOLVE-001 to 006): success, overflow rejection with an unchanged revision,
zero total, negative and non-integer values, the sum rule, and a stale revision.
