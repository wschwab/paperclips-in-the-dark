# Crew world data: licence, scope and sources

Three decisions shape the crew world:

1. **CREW-WORLD-01** made the core mechanics licensed and the faction list user-entered. No official name shipped.
2. **CREW-WORLD-02** (human-approved) adds a modest Doskvol world data set for the crew sheet. It is included as a free fan project, not as an SRD licence grant. See [Setting content register](./setting-content-register.mdx).
3. **CREW-WORLD-03** verifies all twelve districts, corrects Whitecrown's classification, and adds original descriptions and confirmed place names. Attribution and the licence decision are unchanged.

## What the licence actually grants

The [official licensing page](https://bladesinthedark.com/licensing) licenses the
**SRD content under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported**, not 4.0. The
licence text excludes setting material outside the SRD: “The entire
Duskwall/Shattered Isles intellectual property is off limits.” The
[SRD overview](https://bladesinthedark.com/basics) describes the SRD as the core
mechanics.

The [official Downloads page](https://bladesinthedark.com/downloads) lists the
[Factions of Doskvol sheet](https://bladesinthedark.com/sites/default/files/sheets/blades_sheets_v8_2_Factions.pdf)
as a **Campaign Tracking** sheet. It has no separate redistribution grant.

**This is a decision on the excluded setting, not a licence.** The
CREW-WORLD-02 data set is setting material that the licensing page excludes from
CC-BY. The project owner has approved it for a free fan project, on the
community view that a free app with modest setting content and no copied book
text is acceptable, and that the licence and the SRD are linked. Ask
One Seven Design if in doubt.

## What the app ships (CREW-WORLD-02 and CREW-WORLD-03)

- `data/world/doskvol.json`: **48 faction rows** (name, sheet group, tier and
  hold) and **12 district rows** (name, tier, hold, summary and locations).
  Names, tiers and W/S holds were transcribed from the official sheet.
  Holds follow the [Faction Game SRD](https://bladesinthedark.com/faction-game)
  definitions (W = weak, S = strong).
- **Whitecrown is a district**, not a stock faction. A visual check of the
  official sheet confirms twelve Citizenry rows, including Whitecrown at
  Tier V with strong hold. The other four sheet groups contain 48 factions.
- **All district summaries and location descriptions are written for this
  app.** They describe the districts' canonical character in short original
  wording, not tier/hold templates or copied book passages.
- **27 noteworthy locations** are included, two or three per district.
  Canonical names and facts were checked against the core-book gazetteer
  (printed pp. 256–279) and the official Player's Kit vice list and city map.
  Uncertain or fan-created place names were omitted. Each location records
  `descriptionAuthor: "app"` and publication source links.
- The [setting content register](./setting-content-register.mdx) lists the
  included names and their printed-page or official-kit references.
- The crew sheet has a **stock faction picker** that fills the manual entry, and
  a **Doskvol districts disclosure**. Choosing a stock row does not save it; the
  crew keeps only what the user adds.
- The launch screen has an **About & licence** aside with the attribution, the
  CC-BY 3.0 Unported statement, and links to the licensing page and the licence.

## What the crew sheet supports (CREW-WORLD-01, unchanged)

- Quick manual entry: **name + optional tier + status** in one row. Status limits
  come from the loaded game's `FactionStatus` settings.
- **Starting ally** and **Starting rival** pickers, drawn from the crew's own
  faction list. They are explicit records and never inferred from status.
- `faction.set-status` (optional `tier`; omission preserves, `null` clears) and
  `faction.set-starting` (sets `{ally, rival}` together; no side effects).
