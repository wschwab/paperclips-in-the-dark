# Crew world data: verified scope and options

**CREW-WORLD-01 decision: use licensed mechanics, not the excluded setting.**
The human confirmed this boundary after reading the official licensing page.
No official faction catalogue, district list, district description or noteworthy
location is being added to application game data by this card.

## What the licence actually grants

The [official licensing page](https://bladesinthedark.com/licensing) licenses the
**SRD content under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported**, not 4.0. It
expressly excludes setting material outside that SRD and states: “The entire
Duskwall/Shattered Isles intellectual property is off limits.” The
[SRD overview](https://bladesinthedark.com/basics) describes its scope as the
core mechanics. A public download is not, by itself, an additional licence.

The [official Downloads page](https://bladesinthedark.com/downloads) lists the
[Factions of Doskvol PDF](https://bladesinthedark.com/sites/default/files/sheets/blades_sheets_v8_2_Factions.pdf)
as a **Campaign Tracking** sheet, not as a separately licensed SRD gazetteer.
The sheet was read as text and rendered for visual inspection: it supplies
names, tiers and holds, but no separate redistribution grant, descriptions or
noteworthy locations. Its availability does not override the explicit setting
exclusion on the licensing page.

Accordingly, **the faction and district names from that sheet are not treated
as reusable stock application data**, even without descriptions. Nor will we
invent descriptions, silently borrow them from the book or present guessed
locations as official material. This is a conservative product permission
boundary, not a claim that every individual name has a particular copyright
status in every jurisdiction.

### Content that can be reused

- The [Crew SRD](https://bladesinthedark.com/crew) explains positive and negative
  starting faction relationships arising from upgrades and contacts. It does
  **not** provide fixed official faction names to seed into a new crew.
- The [Faction Game SRD](https://bladesinthedark.com/faction-game) supplies faction
  status, Tier and hold mechanics. It explicitly defines W as weak and S as
  strong; those meanings need not be guessed from the PDF.
- These licensed rule passages may be quoted or adapted with the required
  attribution. They are not a licence to reproduce the setting catalogue.
- The README now uses **CC-BY 3.0 Unported** and includes the official attribution
  paragraph, in ordinary copyright-section text rather than small print.

## What this card builds

The crew sheet supports **your table's own factions**:

- Quick manual entry: **name + optional tier + status** in one row, with edits
  to tracked entries. Status limits come from the loaded game's `FactionStatus`
  settings, not a hardcoded range. An unknown tier stays blank; no Tier maximum
  is invented or borrowed from the crew's advancement cap.
- **Starting ally** and **Starting rival** pickers use that crew's own faction
  list, with an empty choice for unrecorded/cleared values. The selected names
  remain visible; they are not inferred from present-day status ratings.
- `faction.set-status` remains the status/upsert operation. Its optional `tier`
  records a nonnegative integer; omission preserves an existing tier, and
  explicit `null` clears it.
- New `faction.set-starting` records `{ally, rival}` together in one revisioned
  write. Nonempty names must belong to this crew's tracked factions. Empty
  strings clear choices. It neither awards status nor spends coin. Removing a
  faction clears matching starting references, while status changes do not.
- The crew DTO's optional `startingFactions` object and optional faction `tier`
  preserve valid older crew documents without fabricating starting choices.

The licensed [Crew](https://bladesinthedark.com/crew) and
[Faction Game](https://bladesinthedark.com/faction-game) rules remain linked from
the editor. There is **no bundled stock-setting picker or district gazetteer**.
The publisher's [downloadable references](https://bladesinthedark.com/downloads)
can still be consulted externally by someone entitled to use them.

## Research draft boundary

The earlier research transcription of the factions sheet (names, tiers and holds) was **moved out of the
repository** and is not part of the application, its game data, or the public mirrors. It is research-only and
not approved game data: it was not imported into the application, copied into `data/games/`, used as seed
campaign data or packaged as a stock catalogue. Its transcription is evidence of the research, not a new licence grant. It was removed from the published history before the push of `ecfb9881`; the removal is recorded in the card's publication note, and it must not be republished.

Its original “49 factions / 11 districts” split is provisional, not an
authoritative product taxonomy. Visual inspection shows **12 Citizenry rows**
(including Whitecrown) alongside 48 rows in the other four groups. The initial
split singled out the first Citizenry row; that layout judgement must not be
promoted as official setting structure. No descriptions or locations were
verified, and none are supplied.


Where official names already appear in the repository, and the steps to fold the private draft back in, see [Setting content register](./setting-content-register.mdx).

## Options for the human

These are **options, not pending requirements of this card**:

1. **Ask One Seven Design for permission.** Obtain an explicit grant covering
   the desired names, statistics, district descriptions and locations, plus
   public redistribution and attribution terms. The
   [licensing page](https://bladesinthedark.com/licensing) supplies the publisher's
   contact route. A future grant must be recorded before bundling that data.
2. **Use users' own campaign data, never shipped by the app.** The manual
   faction editor already records user-entered names, tiers, statuses and
   starting choices in the user's crew data. Users can keep their own city and
   district notes in private campaign material; nothing is supplied as official
   setting text. This card does not modify anyone's live `campaign-data/`.
3. **Offer an empty “Your city” template.** An empty faction/district structure
   can be offered without official setting names or prose, for the user to
   populate. A dedicated city-data schema/importer would be a separate feature;
   this card does not pretend that an arbitrary world JSON file is already
   supported by the runtime.
4. **A book owner's private, unpublished runtime data file is another possible
   design.** It must remain local/private and **must not be committed to either
   public mirror or distributed with application assets**. Owning the book is
   not a public redistribution permission. Such a world-file loader is an
   option, not implemented or silently enabled by this card; persisted private
   crew records continue to load through the existing API.
