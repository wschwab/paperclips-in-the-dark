# Setting content register

This register records where Doskvol setting names live, what each location is for,
and what remains open. The licence reasoning is in
[Crew world data — licence, scope and sources](./crew-world-sources.mdx).

## Approved shipping data

| Location | Contents | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `data/world/doskvol.json` | 49 faction rows (name, sheet group, tier, hold) and 11 district rows (name, tier, hold, app-written one-line summary, empty locations) | Shipped by CREW-WORLD-02, human-approved for a free fan project. Split is provisional. |
| `frontend/src/pages/crew-sections/doskvol-world.ts` | Imports the data; renders the stock faction picker and the districts disclosure | Shipped by CREW-WORLD-02 |
| `frontend/src/pages/about-licence.ts` | Launch-screen About & licence aside with attribution and links | Shipped by CREW-WORLD-02 |

Rules for shipped data:

- Summaries are written for this app from tier and hold only. No book prose is
  copied.
- Noteworthy locations stay empty unless they can be sourced. None have been.
- Choosing a stock faction fills the manual entry only. Saved crew records are
  the user's own.
