# C4 — Playtest contract changes (notes[], turf, heal targeting, vice purveyor)

Authorized by the human 2026-08-09 from playtest round 1 (see
agent-docs/playtest-round1-plan.md). This is a sanctioned breach of the contract
freeze via the dedicated contract-change task (tasks/C4-playtest-contract.md).

## Changes

1. **Multiple notes** — `character.dossier.notes` and `crew.notes` are now
   `string[]`. Ops `note.add {text}` and `note.remove {index}` exist for both
   characters and crews (NOT_FOUND for out-of-range index). Rationale: the
   human asked for several notes per sheet, functionally identical on both.
2. **Crew turf** — crew DTO gains `turf` (int 0..6, required). Op
   `turf.add {delta}` (signed; clamped 0..6; applied delta reported). Per SRD
   Development, each turf reduces the rep cost to develop by one (threshold
   12 − turf); turf marks are kept when rep resets.
3. **Heal targeting** — `harm.heal` now requires a body `{intensity,
   description}`: healing removes that specific active harm (exact match),
   consumes the healing clock, and performs NO automatic shifting. Unknown
   harm → NOT_FOUND. Per SRD, healing a Harm means picking which one.
4. **Vice purveyor** — `dossier.vice` gains `purveyor {name, description}`
   (new `vice` $def in common.json). Game data `Vices[].Sources` feed the
   purveyor menu.
5. **Mastery gating** — action ratings cap at 3 unless the character's crew
   (by `dossier.crewId`) has the Mastery upgrade fully marked (boxesMarked ==
   TotalBoxes); then the cap is 4. Applies to action.set-rating and
   attribute.levelup; error RATING_MAXED (already in the errorCode enum).
   Characters with no crew are capped at 3. Per SRD: "When you unlock the
   Mastery advance for your crew, you can advance actions up to rating 4."
6. **Cohort types (game data, not contract)** — `CohortGangTypes` (Adepts,
   Rooks, Rovers, Skulls, Thugs) and `CohortExpertTypes` (Doctor, Investigator,
   Occultist, Assassin, Spy, Custom) added to data/games/*.json for dropdowns.
