## Deferred features

### Retired-character memorial on the landing page

**Raised:** 2026-08-12. **Status:** shelved by decision, to be explored later.

Give retired characters a dedicated section on the landing page rather than
leaving them mixed into the active roster or hidden behind a filter. The intent is
commemorative, not administrative — a campaign accumulates casualties and
retirements, and those characters are part of its story.

Design notes to pick up later:

- The backend already exposes what this needs: `isRetired` and `isDeadish` on both
  the entity DTO and roster summaries. No contract change is obviously required,
  though a retirement date or cause might be worth adding.
- `isDeadish` and `isRetired` are distinct states and should probably read
  differently in the memorial.
- Aesthetically this is a natural home for the aged-print treatment already in the
  design system — `IM Fell English` epigraphs and the trauma-stamp/typewriter
  accents that are used sparingly elsewhere.
- Retirement has a confirmation-guarded UI affordance (Wave 6 SC-F4): the retire
  control, pending-trauma prompt, out-of-action explanation, and undo/history are
  all reachable in the human UI per the frozen lifecycle design.

## Known coverage gaps

Carried from `agent-docs/frontend-vetting/residual-risk.md` so they survive the
loss of gitignored evidence.

- **Automated accessibility scanning** — no `axe-core`/Lighthouse run yet; the
  vetting wave forbade dependency changes. Structural ARIA/DOM probes were used
  instead.
- **Real screen-reader passes** — Orca (L2) and NVDA (L3) still pending per
  `agent-docs/accessibility-plan.md`.
- **320 px viewports** — contained as of Waves 7 (P08/P14/P15/P16): the app bar,
  faction rows, harm-add rows, and long-text containers no longer overflow at
  320 px; the responsive matrix is exercised in the wave evidence.
- **Slow-network behavior** — only a 1500 ms artificial delay was exercised; real
  3G throttling untested.
- **Batch/composite endpoints** — `end-score` and `end-downtime` have UI
  affordances (Wave 6 SC-F4 + parity); `campaign.batch` remains API-only by
  design (agent-first composite).
